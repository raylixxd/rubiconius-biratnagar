        About Us:

        At OTTO, our fascination with design has never been limited to a single discipline.

        With roots in graphic design, we've always been drawn to the relationship between form, composition, proportion, and material. Over time, that curiosity naturally extended beyond the screen and into the physical world.

        Inspired by modernism and the beauty of everyday objects, we explore how simple forms, thoughtful proportions, and unexpected material combinations can give familiar things a new character.

        For us, OTTO is another way to express what we've always loved about design. Only this time, it's something you can touch, use, and live with.

        A love for design, in every form.
