## 3. Reference Lock Discipline

After inspecting 3 to 4 strong references from the galleries above:

1. Choose **one primary foundation** that governs overall grid, atmosphere, and spatial pacing.
2. Borrow **at most two specific details** from other references (for example: one typography treatment and one hover/transition mechanic).
3. Never average five references into a safe middle ground.
4. Record the lock explicitly in `DESIGN.md`:

- **Primary Foundation:** Exact site or disassembly and why it fits the brand.
- **Borrowed Details (Max 2):** Exact interaction or typographic pattern.
- **What Must Survive:** Non-negotiable visual traits that must appear in final screenshots.
- **What Is Rejected:** Trends or gimmicks explicitly excluded to protect focus.
- **Token Role Lock:** Every color token stays strictly inside its assigned role (accent stays accent-only).
